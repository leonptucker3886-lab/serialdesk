# SerialDesk v0.1 — How to use

SerialDesk tracks serialized stories. Data is saved in your browser (localStorage); nothing is sent to a server.

1. **Add a series:** enter a title and optional posting schedule (e.g. "Tue & Fri"), click **Add series**.
2. **Log chapters:** under a series, enter a chapter title and planned date, click **Add chapter**. Chapters are numbered automatically.
3. **Set status:** idea → draft → ready → scheduled → posted. Setting **posted** stamps today's date.
4. **What to post next:** the top box shows the lowest-numbered unposted chapter of every series, sorted by planned date, with overdue dates flagged red.
5. **Edit / remove:** change dates inline; ✕ removes a chapter; **Delete** removes a series.
6. **Backup:** **Export JSON** / **Import JSON** (import replaces current data).

Limits: browser-only storage, no accounts or sync. Not linked from the main site.
